title: What else?
---

I was born in Karl-Marx-Stadt in 1986. In 2014, I set up a co-operative with friends and bought a block of flats in Chemnitz, where we now live. I have deep roots here and it’s important to me to get involved. I’m active in politics and culture to help make Chemnitz an even better place.

I also love going on long road trips, using Couchsurfing whenever possible. I enjoy getting to know people all over Europe, experiencing their everyday lives and looking beyond clichés and tourist facades. This allows me to learn a great deal and broadens my view of the world. It also gives me plenty of opportunities to take photos and discover new culinary delights. As well as travelling, culinary delights and video games, I’m also passionate about building blocks, which allow me to indulge my love of architecture.
